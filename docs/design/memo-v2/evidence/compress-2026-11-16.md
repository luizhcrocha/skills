# Rewrite against quote, with Haiku compressing, 2026-11-16

This file records one measurement. It supports [ADR 0007](../../../adr/0007-summaries-quote-notes.md) and two rules of [design.md](../design.md): the task over summaries that retires a stale kept note, and the words on the count line.

The file gives numbers only. The notes belong to a company repository and this repository is public, so no note text, no compressed output and no judge's comment is committed.

## Question

[The faithfulness check](faithfulness-2026-11-16.md) found that summaries written by session models invent. This test asks what a cheaper compressor does with the two shapes a summary can take: one rewritten line, or a pick of notes quoted word for word.

## Method

- **Population.** The live notes of the custom-mcp-servers store, in time order.
- **Level 1.** 30 groups of 12 consecutive notes, one group from each of 30 equal spans of the store's time range, with no overlap.
- **Level 2.** 8 runs of 144 consecutive notes, one from each eighth of the store. Each run is 12 groups of 12. Each group is compressed first, then the 12 results are compressed once more.
- **Rewrite.** Haiku writes one line of at most 280 characters under today's compaction rules. An answer over the limit gets one retry. At level 2 it rewrites the 12 level-1 lines.
- **Quote.** Haiku names 3 of the 12 notes, and the output is those notes word for word. At level 2 it names 3 of the 36 notes that the 12 groups kept.
- **Judge.** One Opus call at high effort for each case, with the two outputs in a random order and without their labels. It reads the source notes, lists each statement that no source supports with a type, lists the group's most important facts (8 at most) and says which of them each output conveys correctly.
- **Counted as false.** A wrong number, name, id or path, facts merged so that the meaning changes, and an open thread shown further along than it was. A statement that is true to one note and contradicted by a later note of the group is counted apart, as stale.

## What it shows

- A quoted output states nothing false, at either level. A rewritten line states something false in 5 of 30 cases at level 1 and in 5 of 8 at level 2. tape's bar for relying on a summary is 1 in 30.
- Quoting has one fault, and it is staleness: a kept note that a later note of the group contradicts. It appears in 1 of 30 cases at level 1 and in 2 of 8 at level 2. The design answers it in the task over summaries, which tells the session to retire or supersede such a note.
- The rewritten line carries more: 4.37 of 8 facts against 2.87, in 277 bytes against 654. The judge preferred it in 21 of 30 cases at level 1. That is the price of quoting. The design pays part of it back with the words on the count line, which tell a session what the hidden notes are about.
- Haiku follows the quote task without error: 3 ids, all from the group, in every one of 134 answers. A pick is a task that the cheapest model can do.

## Limits of this measurement

- One judge, one run, one store.
- Haiku compressed. The session models that write summaries today were measured in the faithfulness check, with a different method.
- The rewrite ran under today's rules, which ask the writer to give a minor item two words in place of dropping it.
- The judge chose the 8 facts for each case, so "facts conveyed" depends on its choice.
- 8 cases at level 2 is a small sample.
- The selection and the outputs are not committed. The store is not under version control, so the seed (20261116) rebuilds the sample only while the store holds the same notes.
