Design a webhook-delivery module for a TypeScript service. Callers (HTTP handlers, background jobs) ask it to deliver a JSON payload to a subscriber's URL. A delivery retries with exponential backoff up to a limit when the subscriber fails or times out. The same logical webhook must not be delivered twice when the caller retries its own request (a caller crash after asking, then asking again). A caller can later check a delivery's status, or wait for its final outcome. Deliveries survive a process restart (a Postgres database is available). Expect a few hundred deliveries per second at peak.
