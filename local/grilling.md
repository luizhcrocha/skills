---
description: "Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision or idea. Use to stress-test their thinking, or on any 'grill' phrase."
---

- Ask the round in the chat, never in a dialog, ending on the last question. Give each question two to four lettered options with what each means for the user, and the recommendation by its letter with the trade-off in one plain sentence first, sources after. End with **Go with these, or which do you change?**
- Fact-finding sub-agents play the Researcher role ([models.md](../docs/models.md)).
- When the session runs a fleet dashboard (`fleet` on PATH), the rounds go on its page through `fleet state <dir> grill` (flags: `fleet state --help`; wording: GRILLINGS.md in the fleet plugin's coordinator skill).
