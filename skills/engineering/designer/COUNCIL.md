# Under a council

From the council's first message to you until it sends `council dismissed`, the council is your only channel to Luiz. It is the tab `council` in `designers` ([council](../council/SKILL.md) skill), addressed as `council`: `fleet msg ask council "..."` (with no fleet, SendMessage to `council`). These hold with or without `fleet council`; where the CLI exists (`fleet council --help` succeeds), its command named in brackets records the same step.

- **Channel.** Every question, choice and approval goes to `council` (`fleet msg ask council`), and the turn ends on that message, with no dialog. A wait on another design is the council's: it tells you when the wait clears, so you never poll or message other designers about it.
- **First reply.** Send the council your dossier's absolute path [`fleet council register <slug> --dossier <path> --repo <repo>`]. Done when the council has it.
- **Card.** Keep the dossier's **Interfaces** card on the current design version (DOSSIER.md, Interfaces). Done when its first line names that version.
- **Decisions**, on every council message, resume and approval [`fleet council decisions --for <slug> --unacked`]. Apply each. A change to the design is a new design version, which goes to the council for approval when it is material (DOSSIER.md, Edits after approval). A plan or team version made only to apply a decision is approved under it: its Approvals row carries `C-<n>` as Luiz's words. Add the row to **Constraints** and reply `ack C-<n>: <no change | design v<n> | plan v<n>, team v<n>>` [`fleet council ack`]. Answer each question to the council [`fleet council answer`]. Done when nothing is unacknowledged.
- **Ready.** Make your part of the council's staffing your `team.md` as a new team version, each coordinator's mission naming its tells (the fleets its lanes notify on landing), your incoming edges in the consumer lanes' depends-on in `plan.md`, run LAUNCH.md's Preflight, shape the design docs into one change rebased on the landing bookmark without pushing it, and send `ready <slug>: design v<n>, plan v<n>, team v<n>, docs in <change> (<commit>)`. On the council's word, push it (LAUNCH.md, Launch step 2) and reply with the pushed commit.
- **Launch.** The council starts your coordinators. When it sends your rows, record them in **Launch**, set the phase to `handed`, and run SKILL.md's End.
- **Dismissed.** On `council dismissed`, ask Luiz in your own tab again from the step your dossier names.
