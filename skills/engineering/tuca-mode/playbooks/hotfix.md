# Hotfix

**You own the break until it is fixed live and the extended tests are green. The smallest fix lands and deploys in minutes; the full suite runs after the release.** Luiz, 2026-11-18: "we can't keep things broken for hours because we are doing a thousand tests".

A hotfix is for a break that is already there and blocks others: a red suite on the landing bookmark's tip, a broken tool the lanes depend on (land-check, fleet, a hook), or production work failing now. A red only on your own stack, or a defect that blocks nobody, is a Bug fix.

1. Red: one command, already run, is red on the break at `<bookmark>@origin` (a production break: on the production surface). Name who it blocks. Tell them you hold it ([FLEETS.md](../FLEETS.md)), so one lane fixes it.
2. Pick the smallest change that turns the command green. A landing caused it and the cause is not in hand in about 10 minutes → revert that landing: `jj revert -r <change> --onto <bookmark>@origin`. Otherwise the fix, in one change: no refactor, no neighbouring fix. A fix that crosses a function boundary or passes about 300 lines is no hotfix: revert, and the fix goes through Bug fix.
3. Describe the change in the repo's form (`jj describe`), with a body line `Hotfix: <the red command>; blocked: <who>`. Then Land's steps 1 and 2 (the bookmark, the turn, fetch and rebase). Land's step 3 is skipped: one change, no janitor.
4. The light checks, run directly in the workspace, not through the pool queue or `fleet land --gate`: the repro red then green, the touched files' own tests, typecheck and lint of the touched files, and one read of the diff yourself. No full-repo gate, mutant sweep or long review before it lands (Luiz, 2026-11-18). A red the bookmark's tip already has, in code the fix does not touch, is named in the reply and does not stop the hotfix. Done when the repro is green on the surface it was red on.
5. Land's steps 6 to 8: the verdict (`land-check -b <bookmark>`; a FIX record or a push that is not a fast-forward still stops it), the push, the confirm. Then deploy it, asked once at the task as Autonomy says: a fix Luiz asked for deploys without a second ask; otherwise ask at once, with what failed, since when and how many.
6. Measure live that the symptom stopped, on the surface it failed on. Then `tstack:unblock` tells the blocked lanes the fix is live.
7. After the release, the extended tests: the repo's full gate on the pushed commit (`fleet run -r <pushed commit> -- <the full gate>`), the mutant sweep where the repo has one, and the review land-check would have asked for, each a background run whose exit wakes you. Fix anything they find forward, the same turn: a second hotfix when the cause is in hand, else `jj revert` of the hotfix when its red blocks more than the break did. A red the hotfix did not cause goes to its owner, as Land's step 5 says.
8. Open what the speed skipped: the root cause and the failing test through Bug fix when step 2 reverted or landed no test.

**Reply:** the red command and who it blocked, the fix or revert, the light checks and their result, minutes from red to pushed and to live, the live measurement, the deploy asked or done, the extended tests' state (running, green, or red and what you did), and the follow-ups opened.
