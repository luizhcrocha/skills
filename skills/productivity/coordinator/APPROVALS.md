# Standing approvals: routine acts stop asking

Read from the coordinator's [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) before an act a standing approval may cover (a landing, a deploy, a grant, a rerun), before asking the user to approve a kind of act, and on a Revoke from the page.

The user took the recommendation unchanged on about half of 428 asks, and about 120 of them were routine (approve, deploy, land, grant). An act the user has approved once as a kind is recorded, not asked.

- **An approval comes from the user's own answer.** Ask once with a decision whose question names the rule in plain words ("May the fleet land a reviewed stack whose full gate passed, or is red only on tests proved flaky, and tell you after?"). Once the user answered it on the page and you recorded it decided, record the rule: `fleet state <dir> approval add next --rule "land a reviewed stack whose full gate passed, or is red only on tests proved flaky" --by luiz --ref D7`. `next` takes the machine's next K (K1, K2: every number is unique across the machine's fleets), the other letters number decisions, links and roadblocks, and the page links K1 to the approval. Give it yes and no options ("A: yes | ...", "B: no | ..."). The CLI refuses unless the decision is decided on the yes option, was asked of the user on the page (not with the manager, not recorded after the fact) and the user's own message in the chat picked that yes with nothing after it, so no agent can make one up. An answer with a condition ("yes, but never to prod") gives none: ask again with a plain yes or no. `approval list` shows them; the page lists them with a Revoke button. On a moved team it comes from the card itself: open it with `approval_question` and `approval_rule`, and only the user's plain yes on the page, with no note and the rule shown, or an allow-kind on a permission, backs one; an allow-once never does ([PROGRAMS-CARDS.md](PROGRAMS-CARDS.md#the-owner-answers-on-the-card), "The owner answers on the card").
- **Under an approval, the act is a notice.** Do it, then `decision n1 --kind notice --under K1 --title "Landed the parser" --question "<what was done, one or two sentences>" --undo "<how to undo it, commands in a fenced block>"`. It closes at once, posts an fyi to the manager's news (none when no manager is served), and shows on the page under "Done under your approvals" with its undo. Never `--asks`, options or a recommendation: a notice asks nothing.
- **What is routine**: landing a stack that meets the review rule ([INTEGRATE.md](INTEGRATE.md#integrate), "Review before landing") and whose full gate passed, or is red only on tests proved flaky by [INTEGRATE.md](INTEGRATE.md#integrate)'s evidence, a deploy that the approval names, granting what the approval names, and re-running something already approved in principle (the same command on new data, a retry after a fix). The approval's words bound it: an act that needs the words stretched is asked.
- **What never is**: new spend over a cap the approval states (none stated: none allowed), a change of anyone's access, deleting data, and anything irreversible the approval does not name. These are asked every time, approval or not.
- **The CLI cannot judge the words**, so it checks only that `--under` names an active approval and logs it; the user reads the notices and revokes. A Revoke on the page arrives in the chat as the user's message ("Revoke standing approval K1 ..."): record it at once, `approval revoke K1 --reason "revoked on the page (#N)"`, answer with `--re`, and ask again from then on what it covered.
